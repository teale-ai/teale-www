Bring your models to Teale.

Teale is opening its distributed inference network to third-party providers. If you operate model-serving capacity, specialized local AI models, or regional inference supply, Teale can make that capacity available through an OpenAI-compatible gateway.

Provider fit

  • OpenAI-compatible model-serving endpoints.
  • Specialized open models, fine-tunes, or private model catalogs.
  • Reliable inference supply that can join a routed demand network.

How it works

Teale routes demand through gateway.teale.com and presents canonical model slugs to developers. Third-party providers can inquire about contributing supply, routing availability, pricing, and operational requirements.
